## 6.13.3  (dT) and (fT) corrections

(July 4, 2026)

Alternative inclusion of non-iterative $N^{7}$ triples corrections is described in
Section [7.9.27](../ch07_excited/sec_SD3.md). These methods called (dT) and (fT) are of similar
accuracy to other triples corrections. CCSD(dT) and CCSD(fT) are equivalent to
the CR-CCSD(T)${}_{L}$ and CR-CCSD(T)${}_{2}$ methods of Piecuch and coworkers.[[1091]](../bib_references/bib.md#bib1136)

!!! note "Note"
    Due to a violation of orbital invariance, the (dT) correction can
    sometimes lead to spurious results. Therefore, its use is discouraged.
    Use (fT) instead!
