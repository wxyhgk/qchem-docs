## 12.8.4 Examples

(July 4, 2026)

XPol on its own is not a useful method because it neglects all intermolecular
interactions except for polarization, so the two examples below demonstrate the
use of XPol in conjunction with a Lennard-Jones and a Buckingham potential,
respectively.

???+ example "Example 12.37: An XPol single point calculation on the water dimer using a Lennard-Jones
potential. CM5 atomic embedding charges (which is the default) are used."
    ```qchem
    $molecule
    0 1
    -- water 1
    0 1
    O          -1.364553     .041159     .045709
    H          -1.822645     .429753    -.713256
    H          -1.841519    -.786474     .202107
    -- water 2
    0 1
    O           1.540999     .024567     .107209
    H            .566343     .040845     .096235
    H           1.761811    -.542709    -.641786
    $end
    
    $rem
       METHOD           HF
       BASIS            3-21G
       XPOL             TRUE
    $end
    
    $xpol_mm
       1 O          -1.364553     .041159     .045709 1 2 3
       2 H          -1.822645     .429753    -.713256 2 1
       3 H          -1.841519    -.786474     .202107 2 1
       4 O           1.540999     .024567     .107209 1 5 6
       5 H            .566343     .040845     .096235 2 4
       6 H           1.761811    -.542709    -.641786 2 4
    $end
    
    $xpol_params
       1            0.16     3.16
       2            0.00     0.00
    $end
    ```

???+ example "Example 12.38: An XPol single point calculation on the water dimer using a Buckingham

    ```qchem

    O          -1.364553     .041159     .045709

    0 1
    O           1.540999     .024567     .107209
    H            .566343     .040845     .096235
    H           1.761811    -.542709    -.641786
    $end
    
    $rem
       METHOD           HF
       BASIS            3-21G
       XPOL             TRUE
    $end
    
    $xpol_mm
       1 O          -1.364553     .041159     .045709 1 2 3
       2 H          -1.822645     .429753    -.713256 2 1

    $xpol_params

       1            0.16     3.16
       2            0.00     0.00
    $end
    ```
