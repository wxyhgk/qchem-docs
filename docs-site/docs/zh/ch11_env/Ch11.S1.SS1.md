## 11.1.1 Overview

(July 4, 2026)

**Q-Chem** has incorporated a number of methods for complex systems such as
molecules in solutions, proteins, polymers, molecular clusters, *etc.*,
summarized as follows:

- •

Implicit solvation models;
- •

QM/MM tools;
- •

EFP and QM/EFP approach (polarizable electrostatic embedding); and
- •

Density embedding methods.
